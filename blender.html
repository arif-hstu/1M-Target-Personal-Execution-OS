Premium quality gate

  • Product and documented mechanism believable at close range
  • Materials have controlled roughness and edge response
  • Lighting shapes the product intentionally
  • Camera motion is deliberate and stable
  • Mechanism is understandable
  • Typography/callouts restrained and aligned
  • No clipping, flicker, noise or broken reflections
  • Spec-animation disclosure is visible in portfolio/case-study context
  • Full-screen export feels like commissioned commercial work even though it is clearly identified as a self-initiated study
Rule: Premium proof first. Do not compensate for weak portfolio evidence with more generic proposals.
