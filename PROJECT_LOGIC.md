# Project Logic — 1M Target

## Core rule

**Roadmap → Year → Phase → Weekly targets → Daily routine.**

The daily routine is not a permanent document. It is an output of the active strategic phase.

## Source-derived rules that remain persistent

- Keep the income bridge until a measurable exit gate is crossed.
- During the Head Teacher sprint: 20–24 focused exam hours/week and 6–8 Blender hours/week.
- After the examination: move at least 10–12 weekly study hours into Blender production, outreach and digital products.
- Ponnora stays a small controlled experiment unless evidence supports expansion.
- Finance review: approximately 45–60 minutes/week.
- Exercise: at least 150 minutes/week.
- Sleep: at least seven hours average.
- New random projects are not allowed during the first six months except for paying-client needs, Ponnora operations, essential maintenance or defined maintenance work.
- Saturday evening is the project-specific protected recovery adaptation while office remains seven days/week.

## Financial target hierarchy

1. Required debt minimums current.
2. Build ৳30,000 mini-emergency reserve.
3. Attack highest-cost debt.
4. Build three months of essential-expense runway.
5. Accumulate ৳10,00,000 liquid cash.
6. Increase longer-term investment risk only after the earlier steps are secure.

## Job-exit gate

Do not resign merely because the current job is frustrating. The roadmap requires evidence such as:

- written government appointment and realistic joining date; or
- signed acceptable alternative job; or
- at least ৳60,000 net freelance income for three consecutive months; or
- three months of essential expenses in cash with high-cost debt controlled.

## Why later routines are marked “derived”

The roadmap specifies exact work blocks for the initial examination sprint but gives broader Year 2 and Year 3 objectives. The app therefore avoids pretending that later clock schedules were explicitly written in the source. It uses the same tested daily backbone and maps the source's strategic priorities into those blocks. Those later schedules are operational interpretations and can be edited in `data/routines.js` as real-world conditions change.
