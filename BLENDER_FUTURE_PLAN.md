# Blender Track — Future Operating Plan / AI Handoff

This document is the continuity guide for the Blender side of the **1M Target Personal Execution OS**. Future AI assistants should read it before changing the Blender strategy or extending the execution schedule.

## Strategic purpose

Blender is not a hobby lane in this project. It is the main side-income engine intended to convert existing 3D ability into premium product/mechanism animation work, client revenue, reusable content and digital assets.

The intended positioning is:

> **Clear 3D product, mechanism and industrial explainer animations for manufacturers, hardware brands, inventors and technical educators.**

Do not casually reposition the user as a generic 3D artist.

## Why the first phase is portfolio proof, not maximum outreach

At the time this track was created, Ariscope and Arimatic already contained Blender content, but the existing work was not yet strong enough as manufacturer-facing proof for premium product-animation services.

Therefore the first objective is to create **two premium flagship packages** before making client acquisition the dominant Blender activity.

## Package 1 — Premium Smart Door Lock

Target window: **13 Aug 2026 → about 10 Sep 2026**.

Purpose: upgrade the existing door-latch/mechanism strength into a full manufacturer-facing product + mechanism launch package.

Outputs from one master production should include:

- 45–60 sec premium landscape film,
- hero product reveal,
- mechanism sequence,
- exploded/cutaway or technical view where useful,
- premium final frame,
- client portfolio case study,
- Ariscope main content,
- at least 3 Shorts,
- Arimatic 15–20 sec vertical commercial cut,
- premium stills,
- reusable Blender source project.

## Package 2 — Industrial Ball Valve

Target window: **about 13 Sep 2026 → 1 Oct 2026**.

Purpose: prove that the same premium workflow transfers beyond door hardware to a broader industrial/manufacturing product.

Desired proof includes exterior hero presentation, handle/stem/ball mechanism, cutaway or exploded treatment, simplified open/close/flow explanation and commercial-quality final presentation.

## Core leverage rule

> **Production Animation is the factory. Ariscope and Arimatic are distribution channels.**

Do not create three unrelated projects for client portfolio, Ariscope and Arimatic.

Use this leverage chain:

```text
ONE MASTER PRODUCTION
    ↓
Client-facing case study / service proof
    ↓
Ariscope technical/mechanism content
    ↓
3+ Shorts
    ↓
Arimatic premium commercial/vertical cut
    ↓
Possible Blender file / asset / digital product
```

## Ariscope role

Ariscope should evolve into a coherent technical/mechanism 3D asset that can:

1. generate YouTube revenue,
2. demonstrate professional ability,
3. attract animation clients,
4. support digital product sales.

Preferred content pillars include household mechanisms, industrial mechanisms, farm/hatchery/agricultural equipment, project breakdowns tied to completed work and Shorts extracted from major models.

## Arimatic role

Arimatic should not simply duplicate Ariscope. It should function more like a commercial 3D studio identity for Bangladesh/local business use: product ads, launches, packaging/product reveals, machinery explanation, local case studies and premium commercial reels.

During the Head Teacher exam sprint, Arimatic should mostly reuse master productions rather than demand separate original production.

## Premium quality rule

The long-term standard is **premium final output**. Do not optimize the Blender Track mainly for upload frequency.

A flagship package is not client-ready just because it renders. Before calling it finished, check:

- believable product proportions and mechanism,
- hero-visible geometry survives close shots,
- controlled bevels and edge highlights,
- intentional materials and roughness,
- lighting shapes the product,
- deliberate camera/lens choices,
- smooth purposeful movement,
- readable mechanism/cutaway/exploded views,
- restrained typography/callouts,
- no clipping, flicker, broken reflections or obvious noise,
- commercial edit pacing,
- full-screen final export feels plausible as commissioned work.

If a target date arrives and the package still looks weak, fix the **2–3 most visible defects** before publishing. Do not reduce core visual quality merely to hit a date.

## What happens after Package 2

The Blender Track must **not stop on 1 Oct 2026**, but it also should not pre-plan ten speculative portfolio projects.

After Package 2, switch from:

**Portfolio Proof Building**

into:

**Sell → Deliver → Repurpose → Improve → Repeat**

Use this decision order:

1. **Paid client available?** Paid delivery becomes the highest Blender priority.
2. **No client yet?** Use Packages 1–2 for proposals, direct outreach and follow-up before inventing Project 3.
3. **Poor response after enough high-quality outreach?** Improve portfolio relevance, offer, opening lines, niche and targeting before making random new work.
4. **Create Project 3 only when evidence shows a missing proof or attractive niche.**

A strong future candidate is poultry/hatchery/agricultural machinery or equipment because the user has domain knowledge there, but this should be chosen from future evidence rather than locked in now.

## Client-first priority ladder after premium proof exists

```text
A. Paid client delivery
B. Client acquisition / follow-up
C. Improve strongest existing proof
D. Repurpose completed work into Ariscope / Arimatic / Shorts / product
E. New speculative master project only when evidence justifies it
F. General Blender learning only when A–E require it
```

A future AI should not reverse this hierarchy and turn the system into endless portfolio creation.

## Sales evidence before deciding the portfolio is failing

Initial weekly funnel target:

- review about 40 high-fit opportunities,
- send 20–25 personalized proposals,
- send about 10 direct outreach messages,
- follow up prospects,
- target at least 4 replies,
- target at least 2 serious discussions,
- aim for a paid project every 1–2 weeks once conversion starts.

If replies remain below roughly 10% after around 50 strong proposals, change portfolio/offer/opening lines before simply increasing proposal volume.

## 90-minute Blender block rule

When the Today page says **Blender — 90 min**, the user should not have to decide from zero what to do.

The Blender Track should always provide:

- exact current project,
- exact stage,
- exact task,
- timeboxes,
- **DONE WHEN** condition,
- **DO NOT** condition,
- next-session dependency.

Example only:

```text
15 min — planning/reference/technical verification
55 min — highest-value production task
20 min — QA/render test/prepare next step
```

Do not force this exact split on every future task. Preserve the principle of an observable finish condition.

## Learning rule

The user already has enough Blender skill to monetize. A good default mindset is:

- **70%** execute / sell / deliver,
- **20%** project-required learning,
- **10%** experimentation.

Do not schedule broad courses, addon exploration or unrelated tutorials inside income blocks unless the active project requires them.

## Exam-sprint and post-exam allocation

During the Head Teacher exam sprint, Head Teacher preparation remains the dominant short-term stability task, while Blender should continue enough to prevent the income pipeline from dying.

Approximate exam-period Blender ecosystem target: **8–10 hours/week**, divided mainly among acquisition, premium production/paid work, Ariscope reuse and a small amount of Arimatic repurposing.

After the examination, move at least **10–12 former study hours** into Blender production, outreach and products. A later operating range can be around **18–22 Blender ecosystem hours/week**, with more time going to paid delivery as real demand grows.

## Initial service offer ladder

- **Mechanism Mini** — 10–15 sec, simple mechanism, around US$100–150 starting range.
- **Product Explainer** — 30–45 sec, modeling/materials/labels, around US$250–450.
- **Technical Launch Package** — 60–90 sec, storyboard/multiple views/social clips, around US$600–1,000+.

These are starting positioning ranges, not guarantees. Prices should rise as proof and client demand improve.

## Metrics that matter

Track sales and production rather than vanity output:

- proposals,
- direct outreach,
- replies,
- serious discussions,
- paid projects,
- proposal reply rate,
- average project value,
- repeat-client revenue,
- current project/stage,
- package completion,
- Ariscope inquiries/content reuse,
- Arimatic business inquiries,
- **net Blender-related income**.

The most important Blender number is **net income**, not number of videos.

## Future AI rules

Before extending the Blender Track, an AI should:

1. read this document and the main `README.md`,
2. check the current date and active roadmap/exam phase,
3. check whether Packages 1 and 2 are actually complete,
4. check whether a real paid client exists,
5. check outreach/conversion evidence before inventing Project 3,
6. preserve **one master production → client + Ariscope + Arimatic**,
7. preserve premium final quality,
8. use measurable tasks instead of vague “practice Blender”,
9. let paid client work interrupt speculative portfolio work,
10. reschedule unfinished work rather than pretending it is done,
11. reduce project scope before reducing the quality of the core proof,
12. extend the plan forward without rewriting completed history unless there is a reason.
